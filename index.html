the method.

Standard planning maths, kept deliberately simple so every team can check it.

Forecast

baseline × seasonality × event uplift × trend

Baseline from recent weeks, seasonality from the same week last year, events agreed with Marketing (11.11, Black Friday, Christmas, CNY, iPhone launch).

Forecast accuracy

accuracy = 1 − Σ|actual − forecast| ÷ Σ actual

Weighted (WAPE), so fast movers count more than long-tail colourways. Bias tracks whether we over or under forecast.

Safety stock and reorder point

safety stock = z × σ weekly demand × √lead time
reorder point = forecast × lead time + safety stock

z = 1.28, 1.65 or 2.05 for a 90, 95 or 98% service level. Try the toggle above.

Suggested order

(forecast × (lead time + 2 wk review) + safety stock) − (on hand + on order)

Rounded up to the supplier MOQ. Excess stock is any unit above 26 weeks of cover, valued at cost.
